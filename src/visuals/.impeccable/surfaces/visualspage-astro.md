## Scope

Experience mode: a one-page portfolio where the photographs lead and the interface recedes, ending in one action,
getting in touch. Audience: Saudi e-commerce brands judging shoe and product photography for their listings. Proof is
the work itself (four style groups, every angle, before/after, three brands, a cinematography reel); there is no
other proof and none may be invented. Constraints: Arabic, right to left, phones first, down-scroll only, standalone
from the portfolio. Copy is placeholders until the copywriter delivers; media is grey stand-ins until uploaded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A photographer's contact sheet on a light table: frames in ruled columns with small labels; refuses the
category default of a dark full-bleed hero slider with carousels and a coloured accent.
OWN-WORLD: Light grey ground, black 1px hairlines, greys only, IBM Plex Sans Arabic light/regular, sharp corners,
black squares as marks, one black band for cinematography; recognisable from the full-height column lines alone.
STORY: See the work and its range, see that it is what a store listing needs, see the brands, then message Salman.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin bar; a ruled intro block with four column lines; the name set huge beside a black square;
the main photograph beginning below; the request row follows directly under the photograph.
FORM: The "Columns" sample, chosen by the user from three hand-built mobile samples (after obys grids and gallery,
and Frame & Form). seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Authority

The user chose sample C ("Columns") on the design canvas over a bordered-blocks sample and a condensed-type sample,
and set the identity: sharp corners, sections and blocks drawn with lines, minimal colour, nothing glowing, phones
first, no sideways scrolling. The user then asked for the page to be "a bit live", which added the scroll-in reveals
and the marquee. References the user gave: adidasarena.com, insert-quilt-pony.figma.site,
appeal-rice-stuff.figma.site, themessagetoukraine.obys.agency/gallery, grids.obys.agency.

## Built surface

Built in Astro: header bar; opening (column-line intro, name, main photograph, request row); marquee; work in four
groups with label/value rows and a two-to-four-column staggered gallery; every angle (8 squares); before/after;
brands (3); how a shoot runs (4 steps); cinematography (reel, LOG and graded frames, DaVinci Resolve caption);
contact (invitation, email, WhatsApp and Instagram when set); footer bar. Breakpoints 720px and 1200px. Checked at
390, 768 and 1440px wide: no sideways overflow, the font loads, all reveals fire on scroll, reduced motion shows
everything still. No finish review has been run yet.
