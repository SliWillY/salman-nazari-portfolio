Salman is a multidisciplinary designer focused on creating engaging experiences, interactive systems, and human-centered products. His background spans game design, 3D art, UX, e-commerce, gamification, and behavioral design, giving him a systems-oriented approach to understanding how people interact with designed environments.

With a foundation in game design and 3D art, Salman creates immersive environments and story worlds through composition, lighting, spatial design, and strong visual direction. He works from blockout to final using modular workflows, props, materials, and real-time optimization across Blender, Unity, and Unreal Engine.

His interests have expanded from what an experience looks like to how it works: how users move through it, what motivates them, where friction occurs, and how systems can encourage meaningful behavior.

He brings a technical foundation that allows him to move between concept, design, prototyping, and implementation, with experience in C#, Python, SQL, Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender, web technologies, and Adobe Creative Suite.
